**How I checked.** Most pages were read through a fetch tool that returns the page's quoted sentences. Sites that refused that tool (Britannica, UNESCO, Museums Victoria, National Museum of Australia, Smithsonian, Time and Date, CSIRO) I opened in a real browser pane and read the page text. **Limits, said plainly:** I read the part of each page that matters, not every word; a page can change or be moved later; "loads for me today" is not a promise it loads on your phone; and I could not open Guinness World Records, classical-music.com or the NFSA page (blocked or empty), so those are not used.
